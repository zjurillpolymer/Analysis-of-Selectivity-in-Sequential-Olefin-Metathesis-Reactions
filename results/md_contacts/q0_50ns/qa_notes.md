# Figure and data QA

- Quantitative source tables are stored in `source_data/` and are regenerated by the analysis script.
- The figure uses one restrained blue/teal/violet family; red is reserved for the C-arm bottleneck and stopping thresholds.
- The selected Python backend generated every visual export.
- SVG text is retained as editable `<text>` nodes; PDF uses TrueType font embedding.
- Panel labels are lowercase and bold; axes and legends remain readable at the intended double-column size.
- Replica number, uncertainty unit, contact definition and convergence thresholds are stated in the report.
- No trajectory frames were selected, cropped, retouched or used as representative molecular images.
- The 5 ns curves are non-overlapping window means used only as a drift/slow-mode visualization, not as independent error estimates.
