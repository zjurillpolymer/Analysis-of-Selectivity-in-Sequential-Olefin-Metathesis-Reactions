# Figure contract

Core conclusion: Three 50 ns replicas are sufficient for the present vinyl-contact observable and reveal a reproducible but strongly heterogeneous cross-arm contact network with arm C as the principal geometric bottleneck.

Figure archetype: asymmetric quantitative grid with a dominant arm-pair heatmap.

Target output: double-column scientific figure; editable SVG and PDF plus 600 dpi TIFF and 300 dpi PNG preview.

Backend: Python (matplotlib) exclusively.

Final size: 183 mm wide × 180 mm high.

Panel map:

- a: mean cross-arm soft-contact mass on the six-arm graph.
- b: predeclared split-Rhat and autocorrelation-corrected ESS stopping criteria at 20 and 50 ns.
- c: stability of the 15 arm-pair scores between 20 and 50 ns.
- d: arm-level weighted participation, highlighting the weakly connected C arm.
- e: endpoint-resolved leading contacts with all three independent replicas visible.
- f: 5 ns windowed cross-arm contact signal, exposing persistent slow fluctuations rather than hiding them in a global mean.

Evidence hierarchy:

- Hero evidence: arm-pair heatmap and C-arm weighted degree.
- Validation evidence: convergence gate and 20-to-50 ns score stability.
- Robustness evidence: independent-replica dots and windowed time series.

Statistics: three independently reseeded replicas are the uncertainty unit; error bars are replica standard deviations. Frames are not treated as independent replicates.

Reviewer risk: convergence is established only for the defined vinyl-distance contact observable. It does not prove global conformational equilibrium, a unique structure, or a chemical reaction pathway.
