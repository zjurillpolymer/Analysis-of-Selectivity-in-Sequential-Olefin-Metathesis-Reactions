# Figure contract

- Core conclusion: One V3–V6 (A–D) closure preserves the overall encounter propensity of the remaining vinyl pairs but redistributes it strongly, favouring a B/E/F-centred network and selectively activating the previously weak C–F arm pair.
- Figure archetype: quantitative grid with a paired network hero panel.
- Target/output: manuscript/report figure; SVG and PDF with editable text, 600 dpi TIFF, and 300 dpi PNG preview.
- Backend: Python/matplotlib only.
- Final size: approximately 180 × 159 mm.
- Panel map:
  - a: q0 arm-pair encounter network.
  - b: q1 network after the fixed V3–V6 link.
  - c: log2 change in total arm-pair encounter opportunity.
  - d: leading q1 endpoint pairs, with replica-level observations and 5 Å occupancy.
- Evidence hierarchy:
  - Hero: paired q0/q1 network rewiring.
  - Mechanistic evidence: C–F is enhanced 12.2-fold while A-centred opportunities collapse.
  - Next-step evidence: V11–V12 is the strongest and most replica-consistent second-reaction candidate.
- Statistics: three independently reseeded 50 ns replicas per state; bars are replica means and error bars are replica SD; no frame-wise significance test is used.
- Source data: all plotted values are in the `source_data` directory.
- Image integrity: quantitative vector graphics only; no local selective image adjustment or manual data-point placement.
- Reviewer risks: one q1 topology cannot establish a universal history rule; distance contact omits reactive orientation and chemical barriers; the three replicas probe velocity reseeding more strongly than distinct initial macrostates.
